\noindent\textbf{Status.}
Summary of ideas worked out in a separate chat, recorded for history.
Nothing here is checked against the literature or proven beyond the one-line derivations shown.
Notation follows the skeleton: $\psi$ is the outcome map, $p(z \mid s,a)$ its channel, $X$ the agent's history, and $H(\cdot)$ always entropy.
The agent sees observations $o$, not the latent state $s$.

\section{Setup and the four-term decomposition}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Occupancy joint. Draw $t \sim \mathrm{Geom}(1-\gamma)$ and set $(X,S,A,Z) = (x_t,s_t,a_t,z_t)$.
  \item The one assumption doing all the work is $Z \perp X \mid (S,A)$, the outcome depends on the history only through state and action.
  \item The chain rule on $I(Z;X,A,S)$ together with $H(Z \mid X,A,S) = H(Z \mid S,A)$ gives
  \begin{equation*}
    H(Z) = \underbrace{I(Z;X)}_{\text{1 regulation}} + \underbrace{I(Z;A \mid X)}_{\text{2 policy randomness}} + \underbrace{I(Z;S \mid X,A)}_{\text{3 belief uncertainty}} + \underbrace{H(Z \mid S,A)}_{\text{4 noise floor}}.
  \end{equation*}
  \item Term 1 is $\E_x \KL(p(z \mid x) \,\|\, \bar p)$, so the outcome should not depend on circumstance.
  \item Term 2 is at most $H(A \mid X)$ and vanishes for deterministic policies, the theorem's ``the regulator is a mapping''.
  \item Term 3 is the Jensen gap of the belief mixture, $\E_{x,a}\E_{s \sim b}\KL(p(z \mid s,a) \,\|\, p(z \mid x,a))$.
    Sensing or robust actions reduce it, so active perception sits inside the objective rather than being added to it.
  \item Term 4 is reducible only by visiting quiet $(s,a)$.
  \item With random model parameters $\Theta$, the Bayes-adaptive case, a term $I(Z;\Theta \mid X,A,S)$ appears.
    The preferred ordering credits shared uncertainty to $\Theta$, which is removable once, $I(Z;\Theta \mid X,A) + I(Z;S \mid X,A,\Theta)$.
  \item Splitting discipline.
    Keep a split only if its halves differ in control lever, likelihood channel or timescale.
    Epistemic versus aleatoric is a cross-reference to terms 3 and 4, not a new split.
\end{itemize}

\section{Which occupancy is needed}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Neither a history nor an observation occupancy is needed.
    Only the pushforward through the outcome channel matters, $\bar p(z) = \sum_{s,a} d^\pi(s,a)\, p(z \mid s,a)$, together with $p(z \mid f(x),a)$ for a representation $f$.
  \item Linearising the concave objective gives the pseudo-reward $r = \E_{z \mid x,a} \log \bar p(z)$, or $\log p^\star - \log \bar p$ with a setpoint $p^\star$.
  \item The critic is the successor measure over outcomes $M(f,a;z)$, successor features with one-hot $z$ and weights $w = \log \bar p$ refitted every round.
  \item This is Hazan et al.\ 2019 with the sign flipped.
    Minimising a concave functional over the occupancy polytope gives a vertex, a deterministic history-dependent policy, but Frank--Wolfe guarantees are lost and local minima are generic.
  \item Honest demotion.
    With a fixed setpoint the reward $\log p^\star(\psi(s,a))$ is linear, and $\E_{\bar p} \log p^\star = -H(\bar p) - \KL(\bar p \,\|\, p^\star)$, a mode-seeking cross-entropy.
    The practical regulator is then an ordinary (PO)MDP reward problem.
    Convex RL is needed only for free-setpoint entropy or exact KL matching.
  \item Reward is a proper subclass.
    With $z \sim \mathrm{Bernoulli}(\tilde r)$, $\tilde r \in [\tfrac12,1]$, one gets $H(Z) = h_b(\E \tilde r)$, decreasing in $\E\tilde r$, so minimising $H(Z)$ is expected-reward maximisation.
    A Gaussian variance channel gives a consistency or risk preference.
    The converse fails, occupancy entropy is not Markov-reward expressible, Abel et al.\ 2021.
\end{itemize}

\section{What $z$ must be}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Exogenous, a fixed kernel with respect to the learnable parameters, and contestable.
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Grounded, some observable depends on it.
    \item Controllable, $\mathcal E_z(f) = \max_{p(a \mid f)} I(A;Z' \mid f) > 0$.
    \item Disturbed, the world moves it.
  \end{itemize}
  \item Grounding is the likelihood channel; controllable and disturbed are priors over $\psi$.
  \item Biological reading is an interoceptive or allostatic variable.
    $\psi$ is inherited from an outer loop, evolution or a designer, and the individual learns forecasts.
    ``Fear'' is $\E_{z \sim M}[-\log p^\star(z)]$.
  \item Reward-to-noise and predictability of observation trajectories were flagged as weak and dark-room prone.
    Predictability belongs on $z$ only.
\end{itemize}

\section{The representation $f$}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The minimal $z$-sufficient statistic is the belief projected onto the $\psi$-bisimulation quotient, the ancestral set of $Z_{t:\infty}$ in the latent dynamic Bayes net.
  \item It must be closed under the dynamics, otherwise it cannot be maintained recursively.
  \item It is policy dependent.
    The agent can shrink the ancestral set by cutting causal edges, Ashby's block versus compensate.
  \item Compress by the deterministic information bottleneck, safe because $\psi$ is exogenous.
    ``Most of $s$ is irrelevant'' is a heuristic, the compression ratio is a property of the causal graph under $\pi$.
  \item A $2 \times 2$ split of state factors, controllable by $z$-ancestor, gives lever, wasted capacity, disturbance and noise.
    Inverse models recover the controllable row, $z$-prediction the ancestral column, and an inverse model inside the quotient separates levers from disturbances.
    Related are Denoised MDPs, Wang et al.\ 2022, and ASR, Huang et al.\ 2022.
  \item Hierarchy, after Li, Walsh and Littman 2006 with $\psi$ in the reward slot, from fine to coarse.
  \begin{enumerate}
    \item $\psi$-bisimulation.
    \item $z$-successor-feature equivalence under $\Pi$, value equivalence for the span of $z$-functions, Grimm et al.\ 2020.
    \item $Q^\pi$-irrelevance, too coarse because $w$ changes every iteration.
    \item $a^\star$-irrelevance, the regulator map itself and the literal level of Conant and Ashby.
  \end{enumerate}
    Learning under partial observability forces the second level.
  \item Special case $z = s$.
    $f$ is the full belief and the objective is state-marginal matching, Lee et al.\ 2019.
    The decomposition reduces to $I(S;X) + H(S \mid X)$, regulation plus active localisation as in $\rho$-POMDPs.
    In LQG the separation principle holds and term 3 is present but policy independent, no dual effect.
\end{itemize}

\section{Empowerment, requisite variety, collapse}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Term 2 is empowerment spent, behavioural mutual information.
    $\mathcal E_z$ is empowerment held, a counterfactual maximum.
  \item Regulation drives the spend to zero and must keep the budget, $\min H(Z)$ subject to $\mathcal E_z(f) \ge c$.
  \item Requisite variety, Ashby 1956, Conant 1969, Touchette and Lloyd 2000, is feasibility, budget at least the disturbance variety.
    The dark room satisfies it at $0 \ge 0$.
    The floor $c > 0$ insures against unmodelled disturbances and is permanent.
  \item The anchor must be counterfactual, since inverse models go blind as regulation succeeds, as in closed-loop identification and dual control.
    Path empowerment $I(A^n; Z_{t+1:t+n})$ is the right budget, not the endpoint version.
  \item Collapse taxonomy.
    $H(Z) \to 0$ is the goal, $f$ tends to minimal sufficient, term 2 and probing tend to zero, and $\mathcal E_z$ stays.
  \item For a learned $\hat z$, empowerment is the defining anchor, a regulated variable is one the actions can move, and grounding selects which one.
  \item Error control only supports an inverse or empowerment anchored $f$; a latent $z$ needs cause control, so disturbances must be in $f$.
\end{itemize}

\section{Perception, learning, posteriors}
\begin{itemize}
  \item A BAMDP is a POMDP with a static latent.
    State information decays and parameter information accumulates.
  \item Regulation feeds perception, since it must track disturbances, and starves learning, since it removes action variation and keeps $z$ away from the viability boundary.
  \item A point estimate of $\theta$ hides the learning term, certainty equivalence.
    Meta-RL such as RL$^2$ or VariBAD is the genuine merge.
  \item Posteriors are indexed by likelihood channel.
    Observations inform state and dynamics; $z$ informs the outcome map composed with the quotient, a $z$-valued PSR, not $\psi$ on the true state, which is unidentifiable; a viability signal $v$ informs which $\psi$.
  \item Representation is a rate--distortion decision, not a posterior.
  \item Self-sealing pathology.
    Hedging regulation never fires $v$, and a survival-only likelihood gives a survivorship-biased posterior over $\psi$, maximised by ``everything visited is safe''.
    The fix is a graded signal, pain before death.
\end{itemize}

\section{Control as inference}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Do not replace the optimality variable $\mathcal O$ by $z$; factor through it, $p(\mathcal O = 1 \mid s,a) = \sum_z p^\star(z)\, p(z \mid s,a)$.
  \item The reward is then log viability marginalised over outcome noise.
    The CaI temperature is the noise floor, term 4, plus the setpoint width, and normalisation is automatic.
    Generic CaI is the case of a binary unobserved $z$.
  \item Optimism comes from conditioning on the unobserved future, which tilts disturbances to be benign, the worst bias for a regulator.
    Use the variational fix or a pessimistic, Whittle-type tilt.
  \item When $z$ is observed, $p(a \mid s,z')$ is a legitimate posterior, an inverse model, and hindsight is exact.
  \item The soft value is a log survival probability, a Doob $h$-transform.
    Policy entropy conflicts with term 2.
  \item Three conditioning events on $z$ eliminate $\mathcal O$.

  \medskip
  \begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.27\linewidth}p{0.33\linewidth}p{0.3\linewidth}@{}}
    \toprule
    event & posterior & objective \\
    \midrule
    $z_t = g$ for all $t$, $g \sim p^\star$ & $p(\tau)\sum_g p^\star(g)\prod_t p(g \mid s_t,a_t)$ & per-episode entropy, goal-conditioned RL, non-Markov \\
    $\hat p_\tau = p^\star$ & exponential tilt, Sanov & KL matching, reward as Lagrange multiplier \\
    each $z_t$ viable & $p(\tau)\prod_t \sum_z p^\star(z)\, p(z \mid s_t,a_t)$ & cross-entropy, mode-seeking \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
  \medskip

  \item Occupancy versus evidence.
    $H(Z) = -\E\log\bar p$ uses the marginal, $-\E\log p(z \mid s,a) = H(Z \mid S,A)$ the conditional, and their difference $I(Z;S,A)$ is terms 1 to 3.
  \item Product versus sum over time gives multiplicative Bellman, survival and risk sensitivity, versus additive Bellman, homeostatic and excursion tolerant.
  \item The occupancy is order blind; marginal entropy is not an entropy rate.
    Regulate stocks, not flows, and use windows if a pattern is the regulated thing.
  \item Predictability of behaviour is the rate term $I(A_t; X_t \mid A_{<t})$, Tishby and Polani's relevant information, a rate--distortion problem with a requisite-variety floor.
  \item Sequences of $z$ conditioned on a higher outcome $y$ give a time-varying setpoint, allostasis, with planning above and regulation below.
\end{itemize}

\section{Novelty and framing}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The components are known: Ashby, Conant, Touchette and Lloyd, Sagawa and Ueda, BALD, dual control, the epistemic value of active inference, $\rho$-POMDPs, Tishby and Polani, Hazan, Mutti, Zahavy, Lee, Attias, Toussaint, Rudner, Lehnert and Littman, Grimm, Li, Walsh and Littman, the empowerment literature, Keramati and Gutkin, Pezzulo, Rigoli and Friston.
  \item Claims to push, moderate confidence that they are unpublished in this form, search first.
  \begin{itemize}
    \item The epistemic term is derived by the chain rule rather than postulated as in expected free energy.
    \item Two-channel sign structure, $I(S;O \mid X)$ high and $I(Z;S \mid X,A)$ low; the dark room is where the two channels coincide.
    \item Empowerment--regulation duality with a counterfactual anchor.
    \item A policy-dependent requisite-variety floor, niche construction by cutting edges.
    \item Regulation strictly contains reward.
    \item $\mathcal O$ factored through $z$, with temperature equal to the noise floor, and the $\mathcal O$-free conditioning events.
  \end{itemize}
  \item Reviewer line to pre-empt: ``this is active inference''.
  \item Exclusions the framing implies: no predictability terms on $o$, no learned $z$ without grounding, no anchors from behaviour-data inverse models.
\end{itemize}

\section{Planned experiment}
\begin{itemize}
  \item A latent factored environment with a designer-controlled causal graph into $z$, varying ancestor count, controllable ancestors and cuttable edges.
  \item Interoceptive discrete $z$ with $p^\star$, forecast pseudo-reward, $f$ trained by $z$-successor-feature prediction plus deterministic IB with stop-gradient from $H(Z)$ into the encoder, an inverse head on $f$, an empowerment floor swept in $c$, and all four or five terms logged.
  \item Predictions.
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Compression tracks the ancestral set under $\pi$.
    \item The agent cuts edges before modelling them.
    \item A predictive constraint gives a static region; an action or empowerment constraint gives a bounded cycle growing with $c$.
    \item Agents with learned $\psi$ either over-regulate and stay ignorant, or take damage to learn.
  \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

\section{Links to the skeleton}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Fully observed, with $X = S$, term 3 vanishes and terms 1 and 2 add up to $I(Z;S,A)$.
    The four terms reduce to the skeleton's $H(Z \mid S,A) + I(Z;S,A)$, and a deterministic $\pi$ removes term 2.
  \item The pseudo-reward $\log \bar p$ is the skeleton's $\ln q$ at $q = p_\pi$, and the successor measure over $z$ is the value of the indicator $\mathbf 1\{z = \psi(s,a)\}$.
  \item ``The occupancy is order blind'' is the same fact as the skeleton's per-step and snapshot counterexamples, read from the other side.
\end{itemize}
